# Rewrite patterns

Pick the pattern that fits the post's real content. Do not force a pattern. Keep the user's claim and facts.

## Pattern A: The sendable framework (targets copy link and DM share)

Structure:
1. Hook: a specific claim or outcome in the first line.
2. Body: 3 to 5 short lines. Steps, rules, or a before and after. Real specifics only.
3. Close: one line that names who it is for, so people know who to send it to.

Example shape (fill from user's real material):
"Most coaches cap their income by sending 40 DMs a day.
Here is the 3 step version:
1. [step]
2. [step]
3. [step]
If you are a coach making $5K to $10K a month, send this to the one person on your team who handles DMs."

## Pattern B: The A or B reply (targets reply, 5 or 20 with mutuals)

Structure:
1. A clear scenario or take in 1 to 2 lines.
2. Two options, labeled.
3. "A or B?" or "Which one are you?"

Make answering take under 5 seconds. One word is enough.

## Pattern C: The position people add to (targets quote)

Structure:
1. One sharp, defensible position in one sentence.
2. One line of proof or reason.
3. Leave room: an angle people can extend or push back on.

Avoid outrage bait. A sharp take is fine. A take built to start a fight invites reports.

## Pattern D: The story with a question (targets reply and follow)

Structure:
1. Specific moment, with a number or date.
2. What changed.
3. One question that invites the reader's own version.

## Pattern E: The screenshot or proof post (targets copy link and DM share)

Structure:
1. One line stating the result.
2. The image carries the proof.
3. One line saying what to steal from it.

## Fixes that apply to every rewrite

- Cut the first sentence if it is throat-clearing.
- Replace category words with an instance: "clients" becomes "a coach making $8K a month".
- Remove the link from the body. Add a "Posting note" suggesting a self-reply.
- Remove hashtags.
- One idea per post.
- End on the ask or the audience line. Not on a summary.
- Read it as if you will paste it to one friend. If it needs context, add the context inside the post.

## Replacing weak asks

| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| "Thoughts?" | "A or B?" |
| "Like and RT if you agree" | Delete. Replace with a question that has a one-word answer. |
| "Comment YES for the link" | "Reply with your niche and I will tell you the first post to write." Only use if the user will actually reply. |
| "Check the link below" | Delete. Put the link in a self-reply. |
| "What do you think about AI?" | "Would you rather automate your DMs or your emails first?" |

## Never

- Invent numbers, results, or client names.
- Change the user's facts or claim.
- Add hype words or guru lines.
- Use em dashes.
