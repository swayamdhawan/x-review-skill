# Swayam's voice on X

Source: Swayam's brand guide (Simplify to Amplify). Use this for every rewrite so it sounds like him, not like a template.

## Who he writes for
Ambitious creators, coaches, consultants, solopreneurs, and real estate agents already making $10K+ a month who feel stuck or overwhelmed and want simple, AI-powered systems.

## Tone
- Calm power. Clear. Direct. Short sentences.
- Guide, not guru. No hype, no fake flexing, no hustle porn.
- Simple words. A smart 13 year old could follow it.
- Real and human. Honest about struggles.
- Energy about 8 out of 10. Motivating, not loud.

## Words that fit
Simple, systems, peace, clarity, freedom, legacy, impact, build, automate, one thing.

## Lines he actually uses (use sparingly, never force)
- Simplify to Amplify
- Build systems, not stress
- You're not overwhelmed, you're overcomplicating (only if the rewrite earns it with a specific)
- Clarity creates cashflow
- Funnels don't build empires, systems do
- Stop selling, start systemizing

## Avoid
- Hustle, grind, crush it, 10x, game-changer, supercharge, unlock, leverage, delve
- Sarcasm, roasts, politics, fear-mongering, fake scarcity
- "It's not X, it's Y" reframes unless Y is concrete and specific
- Em dashes
- Emoji spam and hashtags
- "Let that sink in", "Read that again"
- Sounding like AI. Prefer a specific number or situation over a polished line.

## Topics that fit his content pillars
1. Audience: building a buyer-ready audience
2. Systems: AI-powered systems, automation, Creatofy, BusinessOS
3. Sales process: simple, non-pushy selling
4. Lifestyle: peace, purpose, profit, family first

## Offers and CTAs
- He sells Creatofy, Legacy OS, and RealtorOS. Do not insert a pitch unless the draft already has one.
- If a CTA is present, keep it low pressure and specific.
- Keep any keyword CTA (like "comment RISE") only if it is already in the draft, and flag it as bait risk in the biggest leak.

## Quick voice check on every rewrite
1. Could a coach making $5K a month follow this on first read?
2. Is there one concrete number, step, or situation?
3. Does it sound calm, not loud?
4. Would Swayam actually say this out loud?
