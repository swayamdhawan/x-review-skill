# Scoring rubric

Score out of 10. Sum the points. Be strict. Round to the nearest half point.

## The five parts

**1. Hook (0 to 2).** Does the first line stop the scroll with a specific claim, number, or tension?
- 0: Generic opener, throat-clearing, "Excited to share", "Thoughts on".
- 1: Clear topic but no tension or specific.
- 2: A concrete claim, number, or situation in the first line.

**2. Standalone value (0 to 3). This drives copy link and DM share.** Could someone paste this link to one person with no extra context and have it make sense and help?
- 0: Needs other context, vague, or only a teaser.
- 1: Useful idea, but thin or generic. Could have come from anyone.
- 2: A complete idea with one specific (number, step, example, name of a situation).
- 3: Complete, specific, and clearly for a defined person ("if you run X and Y happens, do Z"). Passes the "I would send this to someone" test.

**3. Reply or quote trigger (0 to 2).** Is there a low-effort, natural reason to answer or add a take?
- 0: Closed statement, nothing to add. Or a vague "What do you think?"
- 1: Takes a position someone could push back on, but no clear invitation.
- 2: A clear, easy-to-answer prompt or a sharp position with a built-in A vs B.

**4. Follow signal (0 to 1).** Does the post show a clear, repeatable theme for the account, so someone thinks "I want more of this"?
- 0: One-off, off-topic for the account.
- 1: Fits a clear niche or POV that suggests more of the same.

**5. Clean delivery (0 to 2).** Formatting and risk.
- Deduct for (negatives are expensive: report -234, mute -58.8, not interested -43.2): link in the post body, hashtags, engagement bait ("like and RT", "comment YES"), walls of text, more than one idea, outrage bait that invites reports.
- 0: Two or more of those problems.
- 1: One problem.
- 2: Clean. Short lines, one idea, no link in body, no bait.

## Calibration

- 9 to 10: Built to earn a copy link or a reply. Specific, complete, easy to answer.
- 7 to 8: Good post, one clear fix left.
- 5 to 6: Fine content, not built for the heavy actions. Typical first draft.
- 3 to 4: Generic or announcement style. Earns mostly likes.
- 1 to 2: Self-promo, link dump, or vague.

## Rules

- A post cannot score above 7 if Standalone value is 1 or less.
- A post cannot score above 8 if Reply or quote trigger is 0.
- A post cannot score above 6 if it has a link in the body and Standalone value is under 3.
